Terms and Conditions

This is a draft outline, not final legal text. These terms have not been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be relied on for real-world use until they are.

1. Contributions

Members agree to contribute the amount and frequency set by the group's admin, and to keep their contribution record accurate and up to date.

2. Non-payment

If a member misses contributions, the group may issue a warning, apply a fine, or in serious cases, take further action as agreed by the group — up to and including legal recourse.

3. Death of a member

If a member passes away, the group will decide, together with the member's next of kin, how their outstanding contributions and payout position are handled — whether inherited, settled, or forgiven.

4. Financial hardship

A member facing genuine financial difficulty may request a pause or a revised repayment plan from the group, rather than being treated the same as someone who simply stopped paying.

5. Dispute resolution

Disagreements should first be raised with the group's leadership. If unresolved, the group may use mediation, or as a last resort, formal legal channels.

6. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated over time. Members will be asked to review and re-accept any future version before continuing to use the app.
